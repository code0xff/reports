# Uncertainties

- Microsoft's Code of Conduct is a draft, not a finished standard: it applies only to models Microsoft has not yet built, and the company has committed only to "review submissions and publish a summary of changes" — the substance could shift materially by the time it ships.
- Whether Suleyman's "embedded evaluators" language is a genuine commitment to Amodei's specific proposal or a rhetorical echo of it is not something either company has clarified on the record.
- The Trump/Sacks position is framed here as a rejection of coordinated pacing, but neither has proposed an alternative safety mechanism — it is a negative position (no coordination) rather than a competing positive one, and could still shift once "more to share soon" (Altman's phrase, from the prior brief) actually surfaces.
- Superhuman has not disclosed deal terms for the Fathom acquisition; user-base and valuation figures cited come from Fathom's own prior funding announcements, not from this transaction.
