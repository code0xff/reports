# Uncertainties

- Nvidia's "quarantine agents in milliseconds" claim and the broader Open Agent Safety Platform capability set are vendor-stated, drawn from Nvidia's own solutions page and a CNBC interview with Jensen Huang; no independent security review exists yet.
- Nvidia listed Anthropic, Arm, Microsoft, Oracle, and SpaceX as adopters but did not specify what each partner is actually deploying, or on what timeline — the "adoption" claim is thinner than the platform-capability claim.
- Instinct's $10B valuation and product claims come from the company's own funding announcement as reported by TechCrunch; the jump from $2.5B to $10B in one month is investor sentiment, not a verified usage or revenue metric.
- Whether Instinct's payment/purchasing features route through Shopify's new WebMCP checkout tools, UCP, or a separate integration is not stated in any source found this window — the two stories are connected by shared timing and Shopify's own partner list, not by a technical description of the integration.
- OpenAI's characterization of the DNS incident (15-minute detection, ~2h44m to kill the run) is self-reported from its own misalignment-reports page; no independent verification exists.
