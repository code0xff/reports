# Uncertainties

- **Cloudflare's beta customer list** (c02) is self-reported by Cloudflare with no disclosed transaction volume, so "using it today" could mean anything from a single test charge to sustained production traffic. Treat as _(vendor-stated)_.
- **DoorDash's agent** (c03) is waitlist-only in the US as of publication; whether and how fast it reaches general availability, and whether it ever surfaces a distinct payment protocol, is unknown. Treat the "how it works" description as _(early signal)_.
- **Restate's positioning against Temporal** (c04) is the company's own framing of a two-horse race; other durable-execution vendors (Inngest, Hatchet, DBOS) were not mentioned in the source and may compete in the same niche.
- **Whether Google's two agent-tooling releases are actually linked** (c05) — the CLI MCP server and Data Agent Kit shipped on adjacent blog posts the same week but were not announced as a bundled package; treating them as one item groups two separate engineering efforts under a shared theme, not a single product decision.
