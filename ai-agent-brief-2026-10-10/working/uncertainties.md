# Uncertainties

- Google's Gemini agent case-study numbers (Bradesco, SOMPO, Bloomberg Media, Ulta Beauty, Starling, Commerzbank, Hitachi, Tata Steel) are all customer quotes supplied by Google in its own announcement; none are independently measured.
- Asana's 76x/5x figures depend on which model was the "original production setup" (referred to only as "Model B" in the source); the comparison is relative to an unnamed baseline, not an absolute claim about GPT-6.1 Sol's efficiency against a named competitor.
- Goodfire's monitors are live only for Baseten customers as of this writing; whether the approach generalizes beyond the three risk categories tested (offensive hacking, CBRN misuse, reward hacking) is untested in the source.
- Whether Google's "coworker agent" identities (persistent, @agents.company.com addresses) introduce the same authorization and audit questions this brief has tracked all month for other agent-identity schemes is not addressed in either source; it is an open question this brief did not have scope to chase down inside the two-sweep budget.
