# Uncertainties

- **Oracle governance capture (c16).** The claim that UMA token-weighted voting is a manipulation/single-point-of-failure surface rests on news reporting and one investigative dataset (s17) plus high-profile dispute cases (s15, s16). No peer-reviewed quantification. _(interpretive / weakly evidenced)_
- **Collateral asset naming.** USDC vs USDC.e vs pUSD — terminology is shifting as Polymarket relabels its UI. The economic substance (1:1 USD-pegged stablecoin on Polygon) is stable; the exact ticker shown to users is moving. _(vendor-stated / moving)_
- **Reported volume.** Headline volume conflates primary minting/burning with secondary trading and includes wash trading; the "true" turnover is materially lower and method-dependent (s05, s18). Any volume number is an upper bound, not turnover. _(contested)_
- **Augur current status.** v2 (REPv2) is the documented architecture (s12); 2025 "Lituus" revival is early-stage and may change the oracle/forking design. _(early signal)_
- **Polymarket closed components.** The off-chain matching engine and relayer are not open-source; technical claims about them rely on Polymarket's own docs and third-party reverse-engineering write-ups. _(vendor-stated)_
