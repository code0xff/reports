# Uncertainties
- The "one million merchants" figure and the opt-out path come from one trade article; Shopify has not been read directly, and the article's own context on OpenAI's checkout is unsourced.
- Danske's result is a controlled proof of concept, one transaction on one merchant platform owned by Mastercard. It says nothing yet about issuer approval rates or dispute handling for agent-initiated payments.
- GoCardless's 64% consumer-openness figure is the vendor's own survey number.
- Google's Gemini calling feature is a small-scale test; regional and plan availability may change.
- The API Gateway auth behavior is as documented by Google in preview and may change before GA.
