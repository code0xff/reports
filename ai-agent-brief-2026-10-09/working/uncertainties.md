# Uncertainties

- Whether PAP gains real merchant adoption beyond its seven launch partners, or stays a press-release-stage standard like several predecessors on this beat, is unknown until the v0.1 spec and reference implementation ship.
- Whether x402-foundation/x402's maintainers configure an Actions policy exception before November 2, or let `check-verified-commits` quietly stop running, is unresolved — the issue is three days old with no response.
- Cloudflare's Managed Defense beta reports no measured false-positive or false-negative rate for its new pipeline; whether the "passive" classification for high-volume noise ever suppresses a real incident is not addressed in the company's own post.
- Whether EU strong customer authentication rules (flagged by The Next Web as having no exemption for agent-initiated payments) will force PAP's payments extension to look different from Visa's or x402's card work is an open legal question, not a technical one this brief can resolve.
