# Uncertainties

- The Cursor/Anthropic extension incident's exact timeline varies by outlet: reported duration ranges from about 11 to 12 hours, and reported start/end times differ by outlet depending on timezone conversion. This brief uses BankInfoSecurity's figures (roughly 11 hours, published 2026-10-30) as the single most directly attributed account, but treats the precise hour count as approximate.
- Cursor's "at most 5,000 users" exposure figure is Cursor's own count, not independently audited — vendor-stated.
- Whether mppx's #1139/#1140 fixes, or pay-kit's #407/#408 fix, are themselves complete is unconfirmed; both projects have a recent history (noted in prior editions of this brief) of a first fix needing a follow-up correction within hours to days.
- UCP PR #998's residual-risk framing (address recycling and reassignment) is the project's own characterization of what risk the exception accepts; this brief has not independently assessed how exploitable that residual risk is in practice.
