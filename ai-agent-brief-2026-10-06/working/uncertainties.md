# Uncertainties

- Mohiuddin's claim that the SSRF/trust-boundary bug is "structural" rests on five confirmed cases; five is suggestive of a pattern, not proof that every MCP server implementation shares the flaw. It is also his own four-month-old prediction being validated by his own follow-up research, not an independent party setting out to test it.
- Whether x402's auth-capture feature sees any production use before its first security review is unknown; the project's own track record this month (two correctness bugs in the batch-settlement feature within 72 hours of shipping) is reason to treat a freshly merged payment primitive as unaudited until proven otherwise.
- Wikimedia's phrasing is "possibly tied to" and "potentially malicious" — the Foundation has not asserted a confirmed causal link between the May outage and the specific rogue-agent cohort disclosed in September, only a plausible connection under investigation.
