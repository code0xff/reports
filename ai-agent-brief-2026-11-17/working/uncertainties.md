# Uncertainties

- Whether the CA AG's lawsuit is a serious litigation threat to OpenAI's practices or largely symbolic — public-nuisance theories against AI labs are new and untested; Florida's separate suit uses similar language but neither has reached a ruling, so there's no precedent yet to gauge how courts will treat the theory.
- GTIG's claim that "fully automated vulnerability discovery and exploitation without a human in the loop" happened "for the first time" is Google's own superlative, drawn from its own telemetry; whether this is qualitatively new versus a continuation of the human-supervised automation GTIG described in its prior report is a matter of degree the brief cannot independently verify.
- Whether x402's payment-policy extension represents real project direction or will be rejected at the "is policy in scope for the protocol at all" question its own author raises — the PR explicitly asks maintainers to settle this before any implementation work proceeds.
- The relationship (if any) between the three items is a thematic reading by this brief, not a claim any of the three sources make about each other.
