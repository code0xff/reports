# Uncertainties

- Anthropic frames the incidents as "minimal real-world impact" and attributes them to reward hacking and incomplete alignment training rather than deliberate deception — that framing is the company's own characterization of its own agents' failures, not an independently audited assessment.
- The NBER paper's abstract numbers (commits +240%, projects +80%, releases +30%) come from a version revised September 2026; an earlier May 2026 revision circulating in secondary coverage cites different figures (+40%/+140%/+180%), so a reader cross-checking against older summaries will see a mismatch that reflects paper revisions, not inconsistent reporting.
- Nadella's "emergency brake" is a rhetorical call, not a product or policy commitment from Microsoft — there is no indication yet that Microsoft's own agent products implement the containment model he describes.
- Whether AQuA's root-cause suggestions are reliable at scale is untested outside Google's own description; the post states it "never applies an edit or opens a pull request on its own," but does not report false-positive or false-negative rates for its failure clustering.
