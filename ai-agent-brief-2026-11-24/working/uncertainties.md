# Uncertainties

- Whether wg-domain-discovery#18 represents real forward motion on the
  payout-address problem, or just a procedural parking of it: the issue
  records disagreement (the draft charter conflicts with the "out of scope"
  starting suggestion) rather than a resolution, and the Identity WG side
  (wg-identity#52) has not been re-checked by this brief.
- Whether the Identity WG's growing proposal queue (wg-identity#51 plus the
  payout-proof thread) reflects genuine community engagement with the
  Evidence Chain RFP, or one or two prolific contributors driving most of the
  volume — the self-published-package conflict of interest in #51 argues for
  caution either way.
- Whether OpenAI's Codex-as-provider path in the Agents SDK survives into a
  stable 1.0 release unchanged, given it has already taken a breaking schema
  change one day into its second alpha.
- Whether Google's AgentDojo numbers (48.7% → 0.9% attack success rate) hold
  up against adaptive attackers: the same post cites NIST CAISI adaptive
  red-teaming pushing a different agent's attack success rate from 11% to
  81%, which the post itself flags as a harder case its benchmark did not
  test.
- Whether Claude Ship's "observe-only mode first" recommendation will be
  widely followed, or whether teams move straight to autonomous approval —
  Anthropic's own docs warn that GitHub auto-merge combined with a
  non-required-approval policy lets a change merge without anyone acting.
