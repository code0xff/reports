# Uncertainties

- Shopify's "shoppers configure their own agent" framing (s01) is vendor-announced and relayed secondhand through The Verge; the exact mechanism (which agents, what spending controls, how it differs from the Sept 28 browser-checkout opening) is not fully specified in what could be fetched this window.
- Pluto Security's claim that "about 60% of the risks Pluto detects are in vibe-coded tools" is vendor-stated, from the company's own figures as relayed by TechCrunch, with no independent audit.
- The "quiet protocol window" reading (c04) rests on sampling x402-foundation/x402 and google-agentic-commerce/AP2 PR activity, not an exhaustive review of every named protocol (ACP, UCP, MPP, L402, Trusted Agent Protocol); absence of evidence in a 72-hour sample is not strong evidence of absence for the whole group.
