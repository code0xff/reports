# Uncertainties

- Aztec's Alpha Network is live but is, per the team's own disclosure, operating under a known critical proving-system vulnerability until the v5 release; security claims today are conditional on that fix shipping.
- Miden's privacy guarantees depend on users actually electing private notes and local proving — the public/private distinction is per-note, not per-network.
- Railgun's PPOI compliance posture is a vendor framing; how regulators in different jurisdictions will treat shielded pools that publish PPOI claims is unsettled.
- Fee benchmarks (under $0.10 Aztec internal, 0.25% Railgun) are reported as of early 2026 in a third-party comparison and will shift with proving-cost improvements and L2 gas market changes.
