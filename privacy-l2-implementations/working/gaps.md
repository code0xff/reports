# Gaps

- Direct primary-source extraction of Miden's exact account-public/private/encrypted state variants relied on the Obscura deep dive (well-reasoned, third-party); the official Miden home page disclosed only marketing-level detail at fetch time. Treated as single-strong-source where used, with HackMD as a second reference.
- The Aztec critical-vulnerability disclosure and v5 timeline are sourced from a single industry write-up; we did not retrieve an official Aztec security advisory directly. Caveat in Limitations.
- We deliberately scoped out non-Ethereum privacy chains (Penumbra/Namada/Aleo) — they are L1s or app-chains, not Ethereum L2s.
- We do not retrieve a per-handler enumeration of Railgun's PPOI rollout status across each L2; the article describes it as available in 2026, but per-network availability is not pinned here.
