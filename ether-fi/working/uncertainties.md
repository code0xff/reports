# Uncertainties — Ether.fi

- TVL ($5.16 B), annualised fees ($198.97 M), and annualised revenue
  ($48.57 M) are DefiLlama snapshots at 2026-05-14 and will drift.
  _(point-in-time)_
- ETHFI market cap ($373.98 M) and price ($0.45) are also snapshots
  and tied to live trading. _(point-in-time)_
- Circulating supply of ETHFI is at roughly 83.6 % of total per
  Tokenomist; the page does not display the per-allocation unlock
  schedule in text, only that vesting extends "into 2027".
  _(vendor-stated)_
- The ether.fi GitBook export is rich on user-facing surface (staking
  options, rewards, point seasons) but light on withdrawal-queue and
  EigenPod plumbing detail; deep architectural claims lean on the
  staking-whitepaper page and on what the audit-report titles reveal
  about scope. _(early signal)_
- "No limits" on native ETH restaked through EigenLayer reflects the
  state of the EigenLayer cap policy at the time the docs were
  written; subsequent EigenLayer governance could change that
  posture. _(point-in-time)_
- Gate Learn (s13) and parts of DefiLlama are behind anti-bot rules;
  the report cites them as content sources but acknowledges direct
  curl probes returned 403/blocked.  _(access-limited)_
- The Visa/Scroll Cash card description leans on CoinDesk's
  September 2024 launch coverage; the production card surface has
  almost certainly evolved since then, and any specific limit /
  fee / availability detail in this report should be read against
  the live product page rather than as a fixed fact.  _(vendor-
  stated)_
- No independent academic security treatment of ether.fi exists at
  the time of writing; security claims rely on the protocol's own
  audit list and Certora's public report titles. _(single-source
  field)_
