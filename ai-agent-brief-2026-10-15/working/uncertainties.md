# Uncertainties

- The x402 governance proposal (PR #3801) is explicitly a draft for TSC discussion, not a ratified process. It may be amended heavily, rejected, or quietly abandoned — the same fate as other open x402 PRs tracked in prior briefs (e.g., the October 12 delivery-receipt extension, still unmerged).
- The UTA Trust Kit is a third-party community contribution (not from the x402 Foundation or a named payments company); its listing PR (#3815) was still open at the time of writing and may be revised or never merged. Treat it as an ecosystem signal, not an adopted standard.
- The Apigee hybrid MCP support blog post describes a capability that shipped a month earlier (hybrid 1.17.0, September 14, 2026); the "news" here is Google's documentation and framing, not a new release. Adoption and real-world usage are unknown.
- Whether AP2's six-month commit silence (last merge April 29, 2026) reflects a deliberate pause, a shift of work to a private fork, or genuine abandonment is not established from the public repo alone.
