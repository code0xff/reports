# Uncertainties

Epistemically shaky items that remain even though the draft is publishable.

- **Inflation schedule numbers (c20).** 8% initial / −15% disinflation / 1.5% terminal come from a single Anza doc (s17). Widely repeated and stable, but not independently cross-checked here. Marked single-source in the draft.

- **Slots per epoch (c09).** The ~400ms slot target is sourced (s08); the exact epoch length (commonly 432,000 slots ≈ ~2 days) was not confirmed from a primary source and is stated cautiously.

- **Firedancer production status (c23).** Conflict: the firedancer-io repo README (s25, primary) states the full Firedancer validator "is not ready for test or production use and has no releases," while Breakpoint-2025 reporting (s26) says Firedancer/Frankendancer is "live on mainnet" at 20–30% of stake. Both are presented; the safest reading is that the *Frankendancer hybrid* is live and *full* Firedancer is still pre-release.

- **Throughput figures (c21).** Real-time TPS (~1,400) and non-vote TPS (~1,600–3,800) are point-in-time metrics from analytics aggregators (s28, s29); they move constantly and are not audited. The 65,000 TPS figure is a marketed design ceiling, not a sustained mainnet observation.

- **Centralization metrics (c24).** Nakamoto coefficient (19–33) and "60% of validators in US/EU on five providers" come from one low-trust blog (s30) and are presented as an attributed critique, not established fact.

- **Alpenglow timing (c25).** Governance approval (~98% yes, SIMD-0326) is well attested, but the late-2026 mainnet activation is a target, not a shipped fact; at time of writing it runs on a community test cluster only.

- **Vendor framing.** Several architectural descriptions (Gulf Stream, Sealevel, Tower BFT, the "8 innovations") originate from Solana Labs / Anza themselves. Independent technical writing (Helius, Neodyme, Chainspect) corroborates the mechanisms, but the framing of them as advantages is vendor-authored.
