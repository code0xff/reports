# Uncertainties

- Transaction-count and dollar-volume figures are vendor-reported and partially inflated by memecoin activity; treat as ceiling estimates, not utility metrics.
- The Cloudflare-proposed `deferred` scheme has not been confirmed merged into the canonical x402 spec at the time of writing.
- The Permit2 proxy address (0x402085c248EeA27D92E8b30b2C58ed07f9E20001) is documented in the EVM spec, but its deployment status on every advertised chain was not independently re-verified here.
- x402 V2 renames headers (PAYMENT-SIGNATURE / PAYMENT-REQUIRED replacing the older X-PAYMENT family); some third-party write-ups still describe the V1 names.
