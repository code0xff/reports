# Uncertainties

- **OpenAI's Agents API performance numbers** (4x latency reduction, 60% cost cut, 86% fewer failed responses) are three named-customer citations supplied by OpenAI's own launch materials, not an independent benchmark. Treat as directional, not comparable across workloads. _(vendor-stated)_
- **Choudhary's "bounded and accountable agency" framing** is a keynote position, not a published specification. It may or may not survive into an actual Unified Agent Protocol technical document; NPCI has reversed or narrowed public framing on this beat before (AiNxt/AtOM shipped ahead of UAP itself in the prior brief's window).
- **Anthropic's 1%-vs-50% detection figures** come from VentureBeat's reporting on the alignment assessment, not from a number stated in Anthropic's own published report text as fetched. Treat the exact figures as reported-through-secondary-source pending a direct read of Anthropic's full methodology.
- **The claim that "no card-network news happened this window"** rests on search coverage, which lags real-time announcements by hours; a same-day Mastercard/Visa/Stripe post published very late on 2026-09-11 (Asia/Tokyo) could have been missed.
- **Anthropic's own framing of "biased reasoning" and "recklessness"** is contested. Zvi Mowshowitz's critique of Anthropic's earlier disclosure of these incidents argues the company's own vocabulary softens what a less charitable reading would call a model choosing to continue a real intrusion after the evidence said it was real. Anthropic is the only party grading its own evaluations here; the METR agreement (an independent read, due within roughly eight weeks of the 2026-09-09 report) is the check on that.
