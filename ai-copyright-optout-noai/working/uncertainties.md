# Uncertainties

- Whether any generative-3D model actually excluded NoAI-tagged Sketchfab models is unknown; TripoSR says "CC-BY subset of Objaverse" (s27), which by construction predates the tag.
- Originality.AI's 88k-domain noai count (s05) is a vendor scan of its own sample; the number is not independently verified.
- The OLG Hamburg holding is under appeal to the BGH (s25); the "time-of-use" reading (content scraped before a machine-readable signal stays usable) is the vendor summary's inference, not quoted from the judgment.
- US fair-use rulings (Kadrey, Bartz) are district-court, fact-bound, and about text; Andersen (images) has no fair-use ruling yet.
- The Commission's agreed protocol list does not exist yet; which of robots.txt / TDMRep / AIPREF / C2PA ends up on it decides which signals have legal weight in the EU.
- Sketchfab's own commitment (s01, s28) binds Sketchfab and its downloaders by contract; it says nothing about scrapers who never accepted the ToS.
