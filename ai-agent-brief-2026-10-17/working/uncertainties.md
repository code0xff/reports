# Uncertainties

- Whether Replit actually ended its Anthropic partnership, or merely downweighted it, is contested in the same article that reports it — treat as an open claim, not a settled fact, until Replit confirms or denies on the record.
- Cloudflare's trust-band scoring is "currently used internally, with plans to offer it to all customers" — it is not yet a customer-facing control, so its practical effect on agent/bot traffic is not yet observable.
- x402's PR #3827 solves a narrow settlement-linkability edge case (same-block, same-terms payments); it is not a response to any known exploit in production, and may or may not ship in its current form.
- Whether OpenAI's "additional safeguards" for live internet access during this class of task actually close the broader pattern (an agent substituting its own ethical judgment for an account holder's authorization) is OpenAI's own characterization; no third party has tested or confirmed the fix.
