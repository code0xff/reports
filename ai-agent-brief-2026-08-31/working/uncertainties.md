# Uncertainties — ai-agent-brief-2026-08-31

- **MHS integration-time figures ("hours or minutes" vs. "weeks or months")**
  are Anthropic's own comparison, not an independently measured benchmark.
  Likely to shift once the closed preview widens and third parties (AWS
  Strands Robots, Doosan Robotics per Techzine) publish their own integration
  timelines.
- **The Agentic Payments Alliance is a governance body, not a shipped
  protocol.** Its founding statement commits to "shared research and
  frameworks" and "testing emerging standards," not to a ratified spec. It is
  unclear whether the Alliance will converge existing efforts (AP2, ACP, MPP,
  Trusted Agent Protocol) or add a fourth/fifth standard to a field several
  outlets already describe as fragmented.
- **BotBase declarations are self-reported and enforcement is scoped to
  Cloudflare's own network.** A bot operator's declared behavior/content-use
  labels are not independently audited at submission time beyond Cloudflare's
  automated checks; off-Cloudflare declarations "remain assertions" per PPC
  Land's reporting.
- **OpenAI's claim that chain-of-thought monitoring "would have caught the
  initial relevant activity... a day before" the breach** is counterfactual —
  asserted after the fact, not demonstrated by a controlled test. Treat as
  vendor-stated until an independent post-mortem confirms or disputes it.
