# Uncertainties

- **Mastercard's "30+ companies" backing figure is Mastercard-stated**, repeated
  verbatim across trade coverage of the Agent Connect launch without independent
  enumeration or verification of what "backing" means for each named company
  (Stripe, Coinbase, Adyen, Checkout.com, Cloudflare, OKX, Ripple, Solana
  Foundation). Treat as _(vendor-stated)_.
- **NPCI's AiNxt/AtOM launch has no NPCI-authored primary source in this sweep** —
  every account traces to reporters present at or briefed on Global Fintech Fest
  2026, not to an NPCI press release or blog post found by this harness. The
  mechanics (BYOM, four AiNxt components, AtOM's audit-trail claims) are
  consistent across three outlets but ultimately originate from the same event
  briefing.
- **Cymphony's 85,000-file and "double-digit enterprise customers" figures are
  company-stated**, relayed through TechCrunch's reporting rather than
  independently audited. The company is one day old as a publicly known entity;
  its customer list (KKR, Syngenta, Cass Information Systems, Athennian) has not
  been cross-checked against those companies' own statements.
- **NPCI's Unified Agent Protocol is an early signal, not a shipped product** —
  every mention found this cycle, including the day-of GFF coverage, still uses
  "expected to launch" language. Nothing here should be read as confirming the
  protocol exists in a form ready for deployment.
