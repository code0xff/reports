# Claims

- c01: Mastercard launched Agent Connect on 2026-09-09, giving merchants a single integration point through which AI shopping agents, digital platforms, and payment providers can discover products, build carts, and route customer-approved payments, replacing the need for a separate integration per AI platform.
- c02: Mastercard's expanded Agent Suite for Merchants lets businesses expose merchant-controlled product, price, availability, and fulfillment data to third-party AI models and agent platforms while retaining control over how that data is represented and used.
- c03: NPCI launched two agentic AI platforms — AiNxt (a bring-your-own-model agent development stack) and AtOM (agentic orchestration for UPI partner onboarding and certification) — at Global Fintech Fest 2026 on 2026-09-09, alongside an NVIDIA-built open reinforcement-learning environment for training banking agents on synthetic data.
- c04: As of 2026-09-09, NPCI's previously reported "Unified Agent Protocol" for autonomous UPI payments had still not been formally launched or confirmed by NPCI, despite Global Fintech Fest running through 2026-09-11 and despite NPCI using the same event to ship adjacent agent infrastructure (AiNxt, AtOM).
- c05: Cymphony emerged from stealth on 2026-09-09 with $30M in funding ($25M Series A co-led by Sequoia Capital and SMBC Fin Atlas Beyond Fund, plus a prior undisclosed Sequoia seed), valuing the company above $100M, to sell enterprise visibility into what AI agents and other nonhuman identities can access.
- c06: Cymphony's product found approximately 85,000 files exposed to AI tools and agents at one U.S. public company customer, illustrating the scale of unmanaged nonhuman-identity access inside enterprises adopting agents.
- c07 (why it matters): This window's shipped infrastructure — a commerce connector, an agent dev platform, an identity-visibility product — all address how agents *reach* systems and data, while the protocols meant to bound what agents are *authorized* to do once they reach those systems (UCP, UAP, x402/MPP/AP2/ACP per the prior brief's Tamarin findings) remain either unlaunched, in comment-period, or freshly shown to have consistency gaps.
- c08 (counter-evidence): Independent consumer research (Checkout.com, June 2026) found consumer trust in agentic commerce lags the infrastructure being built for it — 27% trust no organization to operate an AI shopping agent, and willingness to delegate purchases splits sharply by category (41% groceries vs. 15% financial services) — a check on any reading of this window's vendor infrastructure as itself evidence of adoption.
