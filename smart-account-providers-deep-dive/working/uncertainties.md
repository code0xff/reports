# Uncertainties

- **Kernel market-share claim** ("60–80% of AA wallets", "3 million accounts") — vendor-stated by ZeroDev itself [s05, s08]; cross-referenced loosely by Pimlico's comparison page [s33] but not independently audited.
- **Biconomy "1.5M+ smart accounts deployed"** — vendor-stated [s11].
- **Crossmint MiCA CASP authorization** — sourced through Crossmint's own comparative content [s35]; not corroborated by a regulator's public registry in this pass.
- **AgentKit's "framework-agnostic" claim** — accurate at README level [s21], but `WalletProvider` quality varies between CDP, Privy, ZeroDev, and Viem; integration parity has not been independently benchmarked.
- **ZeroDev "first audited account for ERC-7579"** — vendor claim [s08]; not independently verified against other 7579-compatible audited accounts.
- **EIP-7702 long-term security model** — still maturing; Safe's 7702 page calls EIP-7702 "a step towards account abstraction" [s02].
