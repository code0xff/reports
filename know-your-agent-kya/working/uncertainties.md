# Uncertainties

- "KYA" is a marketing label with at least four different referents: Skyfire's JWT (agent owner info), Sumsub's human-binding product, DIF's DID/VC spec (KYA-OS), and Mastercard's registration step inside Agent Pay. The draft keeps them separate; any sentence that says "KYA does X" without a subject should be read as one vendor's X.
- Adoption claims (Skyfire "60% of the web", DIF "meaningful increase in members", Mastercard "registered agents") are all self-reported.
- Visa TAP and Mastercard Agent Pay are described from launch announcements; whether merchants actually verify signatures in production is not documented.
- The academic proposals (BIND, DID/VC agents, authorization propagation) are proposals; none reports a production deployment.
- AI Identity gaps paper (s22) is an April 2026 survey; its "none adequately address" verdict predates FIDO's April 28 WG announcement and DIF's KYA-OS by days to weeks.
