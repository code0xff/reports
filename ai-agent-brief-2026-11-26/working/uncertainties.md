# Uncertainties

- The Bazaar listing audit (s02) is one contributor's self-run probe, not a
  Foundation-sanctioned measurement. The author's own first pass overstated
  the unreachable-row rate (21.5%) and had to be corrected down to 5.0% after
  re-probing with each row's declared method — a reminder that the probe
  methodology itself is still shaking out. Coinbase, which operates Bazaar,
  has not responded to or corroborated the findings as of this writing.
- Cloudflare's "Search for Agents" (s01) is vendor-stated for both the GA
  claim and the framing of why it matters to agents; no independent adoption
  or usage data exists yet one day after launch.
- The multi-payee splits feature (s03) merged into x402's SDKs on November
  24, but whether any facilitator has actually turned on `maxSplits` in
  production is unverified — the PR shows the capability shipping into the
  SDKs, not yet observed in live traffic.
