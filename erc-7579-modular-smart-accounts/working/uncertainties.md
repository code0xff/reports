# Uncertainties — ERC-7579 Modular Smart Accounts

Register of what stays epistemically shaky even if the draft ships.

- **Spec status / versioning.** ERC-7579 and its sibling ERCs (7484, 7739, 7710/7715) move
  fast; module type IDs and minor interface details have been extended over time. This is a
  2026-06 snapshot — verify against the live EIP before implementing.
- **Vendor-led framing.** Most "minimal / unopinionated / unified standard" language comes
  from the standard's own authors and implementers (Rhinestone, Biconomy, ZeroDev). Mark
  such claims interpretive / vendor-stated; seek independent corroboration where possible.
- **Adoption counts.** Figures like Kernel "3M+ accounts" or "most widely used modular
  account" are vendor-stated marketing; treat as vendor-stated.
- **6900 vs 7579 outcome.** The narrative that 7579 "won" adoption is interpretive and
  time-bound; ERC-6900 also evolved (v0.8+) — present as a snapshot, not a verdict.
- **Audit completeness.** "Audited" does not mean bug-free; audit scope/recency varies per
  implementation. Cite specific audits where found; avoid overclaiming security.
- **6900 vs 7579 balance.** ERC-6900's prescriptiveness (per-module storage namespacing,
  stricter validation sub-types) is a deliberate safety trade-off, not pure overhead; the
  draft now presents both sides (§5). Which "wins" is interpretive and time-bound.
