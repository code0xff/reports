# Uncertainties

- Pay-per-crawl is still closed beta (s10) fourteen months after launch; Cloudflare's own July 2026 framing ("a real start, but crawling is a crude measure of value", s27) is the closest thing to a verdict, and it is the vendor's.
- No publisher has published pay-per-crawl revenue. Stack Overflow's only reported effect is that crawlers left (s09). The Leaky Paywall estimate and the "$50k–200k/month for high-traffic sites" figure circulating in trade blogs are both unsourced arithmetic.
- Crawl-to-refer ratios swing by an order of magnitude between Cloudflare posts (Anthropic 70,900:1 in June 2025 vs 38,065:1 in July 2025) and depend on Referer headers that native apps do not send (s28). They are directional, not precise.
- Pay Per Use's attribution mechanism (how "appears in an answer" is measured and audited) is not described in any fetched source beyond partner-specific arrangements with Ceramic.ai and You.com.
- The September 15, 2026 mixed-use default block applies to new zones and unchanged free-tier settings; how many existing publishers opt in is unknown.
- Leaky Paywall is a paywall-plugin vendor; its recommendation (registration walls, newsletters) is its product category.
